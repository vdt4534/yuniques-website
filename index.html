E-commerce advisory

Selective

YuniqueS advises a select number of companies starting or scaling e-commerce. The advice comes from operating, not theory: nine years running our own stores, an agency's worth of paid-media spend, and the unglamorous parts — logistics, support, cash flow.

Recent work includes helping an established consumer brand stand up its e-commerce operation from scratch. Engagements are scoped, hands-on and honest about what will and won't move the needle.

  • Paid acquisition on Meta, Google and TikTok
  • Conversion, retention and unit economics
  • AI-enabled workflows for lean teams
